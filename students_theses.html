Doctoral Thesis (D.Sc.) — 2024

Leakage Detection in a Real Water Distribution Network Through a Federated Prototype-Based Model

Degree: Doctor of Science (D.Sc.) in Teleinformatics Engineering
Institution: Federal University of Ceará (UFC), Department of Teleinformatics Engineering (DETI / PPGETI)
Defense Date: January 2024
Advisor: Prof. Charles Casimiro Cavalcante (UFC)
Co-advisor: Prof. Carlo Fischione (KTH Royal Institute of Technology, Stockholm, Sweden)
Direct Download / Repository: [PDF at UFC Repository]

Summary: This thesis investigates the detection and classification of leakage events in municipal water distribution networks (WDNs) using decentralized, privacy-preserving machine learning strategies. By combining federated learning principles with prototype-based classification models (e.g. Learning Vector Quantization), the framework allows edge sensing devices across the distribution network to collaboratively learn anomaly patterns without centralizing raw sensory data, significantly decreasing telemetry bandwidth requirements and safeguarding infrastructure privacy.

Master's Dissertation (M.Sc.) — 2019

Detecção de falhas de curto-circuito em motores de indução trifásicos usando classificadores baseados em protótipos

Degree: Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Teleinformatics Engineering
Institution: Federal University of Ceará (UFC), Department of Teleinformatics Engineering (DETI / PPGETI)
Defense Date: February 2019
Advisor: Prof. Charles Casimiro Cavalcante (UFC)
Collaborator: Prof. Guilherme de Alencar Barreto (UFC)
Direct Download / Repository: [PDF at UFC Repository]

Summary: This work addresses the non-invasive condition monitoring of three-phase induction motors, specifically targeting stator winding inter-turn short-circuit faults. It evaluates prototype-based classification strategies (such as Generalized Learning Vector Quantization - GLVQ and Generalized Relevance LVQ - GRLVQ), exploring acoustic and electrical sensor signals to provide robust, interpretable diagnostic predictions even under partial or nascent fault conditions.
