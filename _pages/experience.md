### **Lead Researcher (Pesquisador Líder)**
**[Instituto Gridtech de Pesquisa e Desenvolvimento](https://hexing.com.br/)** (Grupo Hexing Brasil)

As Lead Researcher at Instituto Gridtech — an accredited Science and Technology Institution (ICT) within the **Hexing Brasil** group — I lead the institutional technology roadmap, regulatory governance, and regulated R&amp;D project portfolios driving AI-enabled smart grid and metering solutions.

**Key Responsibilities &amp; Achievements:**

* **Regulated R&amp;D Portfolio Management:** End-to-end strategic and compliance oversight for complex R&amp;D portfolios under the **ANEEL R&amp;D Program** and **Lei de Informática / Lei de TICs**.
* **Advanced Platform Development:** Technical architecture coordination and industrial validation across desktop firmware suites, offline-first mobile platforms, stateless diagnostic expert systems, automated production testbeds, distribution reclosers, and smart water telemetry.
* **Strategic &amp; Legal Alignment:** Close collaboration with executive general management and legal counsel to structure, review, and endorse high-stakes R&amp;D agreements and university-industry partnerships.
* **Technical Leadership:** Mentorship and coordination of multidisciplinary teams of researchers, software developers, and hardware/firmware engineers.
