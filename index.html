I operate at the strategic intersection of deep-tech innovation, data science, and governance of regulated R&D projects. Currently, I am the Lead Researcher (Pesquisador Líder) at Instituto Gridtech de Pesquisa e Desenvolvimento, a Science and Technology Institution (ICT, in Portuguese) within the Hexing Brasil group. In this executive technical role, I lead the institutional technology roadmap, technical governance, and regulatory R&D project portfolios—directly driving the strategic deployment of AI-enabled smart grid and metering solutions, supporting digital transformation, and accelerating the energy transition of the utility sector.

I bring a rigorous academic foundation with B.Sc. in Computer Engineering, M.Sc., and Ph.D. (D.Sc.) in Teleinformatics Engineering from the Federal University of Ceará (UFC), complemented by an international doctoral research exchange at KTH Royal Institute of Technology (Stockholm, Sweden) and postdoctoral fellowship experience at the SPIRAL Laboratory (UFC). This background underpins my specialized expertise in Machine Learning, Federated Learning, Statistical Signal Processing, and IoT Anomaly Detection.

Regulated R&D Projects (Instituto Gridtech)

At Instituto Gridtech, I provide end-to-end strategic oversight and technical governance for complex R&D portfolios developed in strict compliance with Brazilian regulatory bodies, funding agencies, and innovation incentive laws:

Projeto REPOTEX — Regulated by ANEEL R&D Program (P&D ANEEL)

Regulatory Framework: Regulated by the Brazilian Electricity Regulatory Agency (Agência Nacional de Energia Elétrica – ANEEL) under the Technological Research and Development Program of the Electric Power Sector.
Institution: Instituto Gridtech de Pesquisa e Desenvolvimento.
Scope & Objectives: Conceptualization, technical architecture, and validation of advanced technologies dedicated to power utilities and grid modernizations. Focused on asset health, predictive analytics, reliability enhancement, and system resilience for transmission and distribution networks.

R&D Portfolio Incentivized by Lei de TICs & Regulated by MCTI

Regulatory Framework: Incentivized under the Brazilian Informatics and Information Technology Law (Lei de TICs – Lei nº 8.248/1991 e atualizações) and regulated by the Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation (MCTI) and the Committee for the IT Area (CATI).
Institutional Governance: Successfully spearheaded the institutional accreditation of Instituto Gridtech as a certified Science and Technology Institution (ICT) with CATI/MCTI.
Scope & Solutions: Multi-project portfolio centered on AI-driven Smart Grids, next-generation Smart Metering hardware and software platforms, edge computing intelligence for utilities, communication protocols for IoT energy meters, and secure cloud platforms for operational and consumer data.
