I am a Researcher and Teleinformatics Engineer holding D.Sc. (Ph.D.) and M.Sc. degrees in Teleinformatics Engineering from the Department of Teleinformatics Engineering at the Federal University of Ceará (UFC), Fortaleza, Brazil.

My research focus lies at the intersection of Machine Learning, Federated Learning, Prototype-based Classifiers (such as Learning Vector Quantization - LVQ), IoT Anomaly Detection in critical infrastructure systems (e.g. municipal water distribution networks), and Fault Diagnosis & Predictive Maintenance in electromechanical machinery.

During my doctoral research, I collaborated with the KTH Royal Institute of Technology (Stockholm, Sweden) under the co-supervision of Prof. Carlo Fischione, investigating decentralized and privacy-preserving machine learning frameworks for real water networks. I am also an active researcher at the Wireless Telecommunications Research Group (GTEL) at UFC, working in close collaboration with Prof. Charles Casimiro Cavalcante.

Research Interests & Key Areas
  • Federated Learning & Edge Intelligence: Decentralized learning algorithms tailored for IoT edge devices, balancing communication efficiency and privacy preservation.
  • Prototype-Based Machine Learning: Interpretable classifiers including Learning Vector Quantization (LVQ) families and topology-preserving maps.
  • Smart Water Networks & Infrastructure Monitoring: Anomaly detection, pressure transient analysis, and leakage identification in municipal water distribution networks.
  • Condition Monitoring & Predictive Maintenance: Stator winding short-circuit fault classification and acoustic/vibrational diagnosis in three-phase induction motors.
