### Abstract

Evaluates supervised and prototype-based machine learning pipelines for leakage detection in real municipal water distribution networks using sensor telemetry collected in collaboration with KTH Royal Institute of Technology and UFC.

* **Journal:** *Water Supply*, IWA Publishing, vol. 23, no. 3, pp. 1115–1126 (ws2023054), 2023
* **DOI:** [10.2166/ws.2023.054](https://doi.org/10.2166/ws.2023.054)

</div>

### Resumo

Avalia estratégias supervisionadas e baseadas em protótipos de aprendizado de máquina aplicadas à detecção de vazamentos em redes reais de abastecimento de água, utilizando dados de campo em colaboração UFC–KTH.

* **Periódico:** *Water Supply*, IWA Publishing, vol. 23, nº 3, pp. 1115–1126 (ws2023054), 2023
* **DOI:** [10.2166/ws.2023.054](https://doi.org/10.2166/ws.2023.054)
