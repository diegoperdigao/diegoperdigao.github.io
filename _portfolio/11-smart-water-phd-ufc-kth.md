### Project Overview

Conducted across the **SPIRAL Laboratory (UFC)** and the **Networks and Systems Engineering (NSE) Division at KTH Royal Institute of Technology** (including a full-year visiting doctoral stay in Stockholm in 2022), **SMART WATER** delivered privacy-preserving anomaly and leakage detection algorithms for real municipal water distribution networks.

### Scientific Outputs

* **D.Sc. Thesis (2024):** *Leakage Detection in a Real Water Distribution Network Through a Federated Prototype-Based Model* ([UFC Repository](https://repositorio.ufc.br/handle/riufc/80329)).
* **Wiley-IEEE Book Chapter (2025)**, ***Water Supply* Journal Article (2023)**, **IWA WWCE (2022)**, **Innovations for the Blue Planet (2024)**, and **CBIC (2025)**.

### Visão Geral do Projeto

Desenvolvido em colaboração entre o **Laboratório SPIRAL (UFC)** e a **Divisão de Redes e Engenharia de Sistemas (NSE) do KTH Royal Institute of Technology** (com doutorado sanduíche em Estocolmo ao longo de 2022), o projeto **SMART WATER** concebeu algoritmos descentralizados e com preservação de privacidade para detecção de vazamentos em redes reais de água.

### Resultados Científicos

* **Tese de Doutorado (2024):** *Leakage Detection in a Real Water Distribution Network Through a Federated Prototype-Based Model* ([Repositório UFC](https://repositorio.ufc.br/handle/riufc/80329)).
* Publicações resultantes na **Wiley-IEEE Press (2025)**, periódico ***Water Supply* (2023)**, **IWA WWCE (2022)**, **Innovations for the Blue Planet (2024)** e **CBIC (2025)**.
