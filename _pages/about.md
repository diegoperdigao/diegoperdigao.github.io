**Diego Perdigão Sousa, D.Sc.** operates at the strategic intersection of **deep-tech AI innovation**, **industrial smart grid & metering systems**, and **governance of regulated R&D portfolios**. He is currently the **Lead Researcher (Pesquisador Líder)** at **Instituto Gridtech de Pesquisa e Desenvolvimento** — an accredited Science and Technology Institution (ICT) within the **Hexing Brasil** group — where he leads the institutional technology roadmap, regulatory compliance (ANEEL R&D Program and Lei de TICs/MCTI), and multidisciplinary engineering teams.

He holds a **B.Sc. in Computer Engineering**, an **M.Sc.**, and a **D.Sc. (Ph.D.) in Teleinformatics Engineering** from the **Federal University of Ceará (UFC)**, including an international doctoral research exchange at **KTH Royal Institute of Technology** (Stockholm, Sweden) and **CNPq Postdoctoral Fellowship** experience at the **SPIRAL Laboratory (UFC)** in partnership with KTH and Uppsala University.

### Core Engineering & Scientific Pillars

* **Regulated R&D Governance & Strategy:** End-to-end technical and regulatory leadership under **ANEEL R&D** and **Lei de TICs / Lei de Informática (MCTI/CATI)**, bridging corporate management, legal frameworks, and engineering execution.
* **Smart Grids & Metering Platforms:** Technical architecture and validation for advanced metering infrastructure (AMI), industrial test automation, LoRa/serial distribution reclosers, and smart water telemetry.
* **Federated Learning & Edge AI:** Privacy-preserving distributed learning under non-IID telemetry conditions for cyber-physical systems and resource-constrained edge nodes.
* **Interpretable Prototype-Based ML:** Geometric and distance-based models (SOM, GLVQ, GRLVQ, and prototype ensembles) focused on explainable diagnostics in power, motors, and water networks.

### Pilares Científicos e de Engenharia

* **Governança e Estratégia em P&D Regulado:** Liderança técnica e regulatória ponta a ponta sob os marcos **P&D ANEEL** e **Lei de TICs / Lei de Informática (MCTI/CATI)**, conectando diretoria executiva, áreas jurídicas e times de engenharia.
* **Smart Grids e Infraestrutura de Medição (AMI):** Arquitetura técnica e validação de ferramentas de parametrização massiva, ensaios industriais automatizados, religadores com comunicação LoRa/Serial e telemetria hidráulica.
* **Aprendizado Federado e Edge AI:** Treinamento colaborativo e distribuído com preservação de privacidade sob dados não-IID para sistemas ciberfísicos e dispositivos com recursos limitados.
* **Machine Learning Interpretável Baseado em Protótipos:** Arquiteturas geométricas baseadas em distância (SOM, GLVQ, GRLVQ e comitês) com foco em explicabilidade para diagnóstico em redes de distribuição e máquinas elétricas.
