### Doctoral Thesis (D.Sc.) — 2024

**[Leakage Detection in a Real Water Distribution Network Through a Federated Prototype-Based Model](https://repositorio.ufc.br/bitstream/riufc/80329/1/2024_dpsousa.pdf)**
* **Degree:** Doctor of Science (D.Sc.) in Teleinformatics Engineering (2019 – 2024 · *GPA: 8.96/10*).
* **Institution:** Federal University of Ceará (UFC), Department of Teleinformatics Engineering (DETI / PPGETI).
* **International Doctoral Exchange:** Visiting Researcher at KTH Royal Institute of Technology (NSE Division, Stockholm, Sweden · Jan – Dec 2022).
* **Advisor:** [Prof. Dr. Charles Casimiro Cavalcante](https://charlescasimiro.github.io/) (UFC).
* **Co-advisor:** [Prof. Dr. Carlo Fischione](https://people.kth.se/~carlofi/) (KTH Royal Institute of Technology, Sweden).
* **Links:** [UFC Repository Record](https://repositorio.ufc.br/handle/riufc/80329) · [Full Thesis PDF](https://repositorio.ufc.br/bitstream/riufc/80329/1/2024_dpsousa.pdf)
* **Abstract:** Investigates the detection and classification of leakage events in municipal water distribution networks (WDNs) using decentralized, privacy-preserving machine learning strategies. By uniting federated learning with prototype-based classifiers (Learning Vector Quantization), distributed edge loggers collaboratively learn physical anomaly signatures without centralizing raw hydraulic/acoustic telemetry, significantly cutting communication overhead while preserving utility privacy.

### Master's Dissertation (M.Sc.) — 2019

**[Detecção de falhas de curto-circuito em motores de indução trifásicos usando classificadores baseados em protótipos](http://repositorio.ufc.br/bitstream/riufc/49906/5/2019_dis_dpsousa.pdf)**
* **Degree:** Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Teleinformatics Engineering (2017 – 2019 · *GPA: 8.89/10*).
* **Institution:** Federal University of Ceará (UFC), Wireless Telecommunications Research Group (GTEL / PPGETI).
* **Advisor:** [Prof. Dr. Charles Casimiro Cavalcante](https://charlescasimiro.github.io/) (UFC).
* **Co-supervisor:** [Prof. Dr. Guilherme de Alencar Barreto](http://lattes.cnpq.br/8902002461422112) (UFC).
* **Links:** [UFC Repository Record](http://repositorio.ufc.br/handle/riufc/49906) · [Full Dissertation PDF](http://repositorio.ufc.br/bitstream/riufc/49906/5/2019_dis_dpsousa.pdf)
* **Abstract:** Evaluates non-invasive condition monitoring of three-phase induction motors targeting stator inter-turn short-circuit faults. Compares prototype-based classification schemes (such as GLVQ and GRLVQ) over acoustic and electrical sensor signals to achieve interpretable, accurate early fault diagnosis.
